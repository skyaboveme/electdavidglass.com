## Biography
David Glass has spent his life building in Bastrop County. As a business owner, rancher, and community leader for more than four decades, he understands what it takes to manage growth without losing what makes this place home.

Before entering public service, David built and ran a successful national software company for over 40 years. He brought that same operational rigor — tight budgets, real accountability, measurable outcomes — directly to Bastrop County government.

He and his wife have been married for over 40 years. They raised their four children in Bastrop County and are now watching their six grandchildren grow up in the same community.

## Leadership Roles
1. **County Commissioner, Precinct 4** — Bastrop County. Managing infrastructure, the county road system, and the Precinct 4 budget for Elgin and rural eastern Bastrop County.
2. **President, Aqua Water Supply Corporation** — Overseeing critical water infrastructure that provides clean, reliable water to thousands of Bastrop County residents.
3. **Board Member, Bastrop County Appraisal District** — Ensuring fair, transparent, and accurate property valuations for every taxpayer.
4. **President, Elgin Economic Development Corporation** — Guiding economic growth to bring responsible businesses and jobs to the local community.
5. **Austin Community College & Regional Transportation Boards** — Positioning workforce and infrastructure for the future.

## Platform & Priorities
1. **Roads & Infrastructure**: Maintaining and upgrading roads as Bastrop County grows at record pace. Transitioning from reactive patchwork to proactive lifecycle management. Dozens of road projects completed across Precinct 4.
2. **Responsible Growth Management**: Subdivision approvals contingent on the developer — not the county taxpayer — paying for required road improvements, drainage infrastructure, and utilities.
3. **Fiscal Accountability**: Private-sector business rigor applied to the county budget. Every department must be tight, transparent, justified, and tied to measurable outcomes. Consistent budgetary restraint to deliver core services without tax hikes.
4. **Water Security**: Leveraging deep expertise from the Aqua Water Supply Corporation board to protect Bastrop County's aquifers and ensure water strategies are built on reality, not optimism.
5. **Community Preservation**: Making decisions that respect property rights while actively preserving the open spaces and community institutions that define Bastrop County.

## Precinct 4 Coverage
Precinct 4 covers the vital northern and eastern stretches of Bastrop County, including Elgin and extensive rural communities. Key focus areas include active road maintenance schedules, community-driven infrastructure improvements, and protecting the rural character of the district.
